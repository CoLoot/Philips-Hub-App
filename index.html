Pairing a bulb
  1. Turn the bulb on at the wall switch and stay within about 30 feet of it.
  2. In the Hue app, go to Settings › Voice assistants › Amazon Alexa (or Google Home) › Make discoverable. A factory-reset bulb is also discoverable.
  3. Close the Hue app so it isn't holding the bulb's connection.
  4. Tap Add a light, pick the bulb, and accept Android's pairing request if one appears.
If a bulb won't connect
  • Not in the list: tap "Show every Bluetooth device" and look for a name that starts with "Hue".
  • Refused to pair: repeat step 2. The discoverable window only lasts a few minutes.
  • Connected before, not now: make sure the wall switch is on, close the Hue app, and tap Try again on the card.
  • Lights disappeared after clearing Chrome's data: add them again. Chrome forgets which bulbs this page may use.

Your phone talks to each bulb directly over Bluetooth. Nothing goes through the internet, so control works only while you're near the lights.

Works in Chrome on Android, Windows and Mac. iPhone browsers can't use Bluetooth. To open it like an app, use Chrome's menu › Add to Home screen.

Unofficial and not made by Philips. A bulb firmware update could change how the bulbs respond.
